CeraVe Hydrating Facial Cleanser

A gentle, non-foaming face wash for normal to dry skin. It cleans without stripping, thanks to three essential ceramides and hyaluronic acid that help keep the skin barrier healthy. It has no added fragrance and is accepted by the National Eczema Association.

Last updated 14 September 2026. Developed with dermatologists.

Key benefits

  • Three essential ceramides (1, 3 and 6-II) help restore the skin barrier
  • Hyaluronic acid helps skin hold on to moisture
  • Non-comedogenic and free of added fragrance
  • NEA Seal of Acceptance

Who is it for?

People with normal to dry skin, including skin that feels tight after washing. It suits sensitive skin and is a common pick for eczema-prone skin.

How do I use it?

  1. Wet your face with lukewarm water.
  2. Massage a small amount into the skin for about 30 seconds.
  3. Rinse and pat dry. Use morning and night.

Is it fragrance-free?

It has no added fragrance. That makes it a good choice if fragrance tends to irritate your skin.

Does it remove makeup?

It removes light makeup and sunscreen. For waterproof makeup, use a dedicated remover first.
